How to edit

• Drag a block sideways to retime — it snaps to the grid.
• Drag up / down between lanes to change its drum type.
• Alt-drag or double-click to duplicate a hit.
• Select a block and press Delete to remove it.
• Scroll to pan; the playhead shows playback.
